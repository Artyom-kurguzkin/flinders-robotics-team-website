Autonomous surface vessels offer a cost-effective way to monitor the marine environment
and collect data at sea, and they open up work in places that are hazardous or
protected — where putting a crewed vessel and its people is either dangerous or simply
not permitted.

**TopCat** is an autonomous surface vessel developed at Flinders University by the
Centre for Maritime Engineering, Control and Imaging. It is the platform Team Australis2
takes to the Maritime RobotX Challenge, and a working research testbed for maritime
autonomy between competitions.
