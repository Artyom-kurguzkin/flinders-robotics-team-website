## Overview

Virtual RobotX moved the challenge into simulation. Beyond competing, Team TopCat
contributed back to the competition environment itself through pull requests and bug
reports, and used the event to trial distributed development practices across the team.

## Focus

Work concentrated on the autonomy stack rather than the platform: object classification,
sensor fusion, and the decision-making layer that sits above them.

## The course

Eight simulated tasks, including station keeping, way-finding, landmark localisation,
channel traversal, docking, and scan-and-dock.

## Lessons learnt

- **Manage expectations and budget for unknowns.** Tasks shifted in ways the team had
  not planned for; algorithms written with some flexibility survived, rigid ones did not.
- **Centralise the repository.** Parallel work across a distributed team turns into
  merge conflicts fast without one authoritative codebase.
